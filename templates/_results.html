How this is calculated
  1. Road is deducted from the gross plot area (plots that need division only).
  2. Green area is deducted from what remains, by size bracket: {% for label, pct in green_brackets %} {% endfor %}
    {{ label }}{{ pct }}%
  3. Buildable area = net land × zone share × density, summed per zone.
  4. Coverage = net land × zone share × coverage. Height capacity = coverage × floors (max height ÷ floor height).
  5. The efficiency incentive adds 5% to the total buildable area.
